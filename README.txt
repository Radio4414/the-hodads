THE HODADS — QR LANDING PAGE

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- index.html       The landing page
- style.css        The garage-punk/CBGBs styling
- images/hodads-wall.jpg   Your supplied artwork
- hodads-video.mp4  PLACE YOUR 10-SECOND VIDEO HERE

HOW TO USE
1. Put your 10-second MP4 in this folder and name it:
   hodads-video.mp4
2. Upload the whole folder to a free static host such as GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages.
3. The live website address can then be used to make your QR code.
4. You can replace the MP4 later without changing the QR code, as long as the page address stays the same.

The page is designed mobile-first because most QR-code visitors will open it on a phone.
